What it does

Knowledge Vault is built around how your organization actually works — as a tree of roles, not a list of names.

Courses attach to roles, not people. Publish the safety induction to Plant → Shift B → Operators once. Everyone in that role gets it, and anyone who joins next month gets it automatically. Nobody assigns training person by person again.
Build the material inside the product. A real editor — formatting ribbon, drag-and-drop blocks, spreadsheet-style tables, audio and video with non-skippable playback, and a present mode. No separate authoring licence.
Set exams and have them marked for you. Pass marks, randomised questions, time limits, attempt caps — marked on our server, so the answer key never reaches the candidate's browser. A hundred candidates cost the same as one.
Prove it afterwards. “They attended the briefing” becomes “they scored 88% on the paper, first attempt, at 14:02 on the 9th.” Reminders to anyone behind go out in one click.
Every document is classified and covered. Public, Confidential, Private or Secret is compulsory on every item, with an auto-generated cover page, scope page and versioned header/footer. Audit-ready by default, not by discipline.
Approvals are built in. Staff propose documents; they publish only after a manager's review. Joining, course requests and visibility changes run through the same approve-or-deny centre.

Four things you will not find elsewhere at this price
1 · Your documents live on your storage, not ours.
Point Knowledge Vault at your own NAS and the files stay in your building, under your backup policy, on hardware you already paid for. We keep the roles, the records and the reading experience; the bytes stay yours. Data residency without an enterprise contract — and the reason the price can be flat.
2 · Your organization's existence belongs to you.
Every organization has a custody file, encrypted under a Supreme password only you know. Hold that file and you can revive your organization yourself. Your training history is never hostage to a renewal conversation.
3 · No email addresses required.
Identity is a username, not a mailbox. If half your people are on a shop floor, a site or a route and have never had a company email address, they are fully in the system on day one — the exact point where rollouts of this kind usually stall.
4 · Exams without a proctoring service.
Full-screen sitting with warnings when a candidate leaves the paper, server-side marking, one record per attempt. The invigilator is built in.
