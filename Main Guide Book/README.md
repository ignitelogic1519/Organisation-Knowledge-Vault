# Knowledge Vault — The Main Guide Book

**A complete, plain-language guide to using Knowledge Vault, for end users.**

Knowledge Vault is your organization's training and knowledge platform. It maps your
company as a living **constellation of roles**, delivers the right courses to the right
people, keeps a proper **library** of your documents, and shows you — in real time — who
has completed what. Everything stays in **your** custody.

This book walks through every feature the way you actually meet it in the product, one
chapter at a time. Every screen you see here comes from a real sample organization,
**Aurora Robotics**, so you can follow along and recognise exactly what you'll see.

![The Knowledge Vault welcome screen](images/welcome-landing.png)

---

## How to read this book

- **New to the platform?** Read Chapters 1–4 in order — they take you from creating a
  profile to understanding the constellation.
- **Setting up your company?** Chapters 3, 5, 6 and 14 cover founding an organization,
  building its structure, placing people, and safeguarding it.
- **Publishing knowledge?** Chapters 7, 8 and 9 cover courses, the Studio and the library.
- **A learner or team member?** Chapters 10 and 13 are for you — My Learning and
  Notifications.
- **A manager?** Chapters 11 and 12 cover Requests and Compliance.

Each chapter is self-contained, with a short **"What it is"**, a step-by-step
**"How to use it"**, and **Tips** at the end.
